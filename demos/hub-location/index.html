hub location validation

A small operations team has GPS coordinates for its hubs that drift out of sync with reality. This runs the actual 2-of-3 validation rule the team ships today against synthetic readings, then walks the result through the review and correction pipeline it's moving into next.

Every threshold below — 100 m agreement, a 2 km line between a quiet database fix and one that needs a human to confirm it — is a real number from that project's config.yaml, not a demo stand-in.
